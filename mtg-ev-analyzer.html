How to read this

A deck ranking #1 across multiple scenarios is a robust pick regardless of how the meta shifts. Δ includes both EMWR and CE bonus changes. Scenarios use the same matchup matrix but shift meta shares directionally.

Methodology
Core formula
EMWR = Σ [ share(opp)/Σshares × WR(deck,opp) ]
CE = Top8_share% ÷ Meta_share%
Adj = EMWR + weight × (CE − 1.0) × meta%

CE of 1.0 = deck converts Top 8 slots exactly proportional to its meta share. Above 1.0 = overconverts. The bonus scales by meta share so small fringe decks with skewed CE don't dominate.

Field size & variance

Variance = weighted SD of matchup win rates. In small fields, high-variance decks gain EV (more chance to run hot). In large fields, variance is penalized. The adjustment is:

FieldAdj = −variance × (rounds / 100) × fieldFactor
fieldFactor = log10(fieldSize) / 3
Momentum

Momentum = 2-week meta share minus 2-month meta share. A rising deck (+) is gaining players and attention; it may be powerful but increasingly targeted. A falling deck (−) may be underrepresented and under-hated.

Limitations
  • Matchup data is MTGO-only; paper meta may differ
  • Alchemy data is approximated (MTGTop8 tracks Historic; Alchemy is MTGA-only)
  • Low meta-share decks have noisy CE — treat with skepticism
  • Pilot skill not modeled — high-complexity decks (Storm, Doomsday) have higher ceilings than avg data shows
