01 | What would you take?
Quick question. When you open a draft pack, how long does it take you to decide what you'd pick? That's the fun part for me.

02 | The fun is in the decision.
I love looking at a pack, weighing a couple of options, and finding out what another drafter would have done. It's a surprisingly fun thing to practice.

03 | So I built Pack One.
That's why I built Pack One. It's a daily game around those individual decisions, without asking you to sit through an entire draft.

04 | 8 separate picks. 8 trophy drafts.
Each challenge has eight picks from eight different real trophy drafts. Not eight picks from the same draft. Eight separate situations, each with its own context.

05 | Make your pick. Then compare.
You see the pack and what that player had already drafted. You make your choice, lock it in, and then find out what the trophy drafter actually picked.

06 | Disagreement is the point.
Sometimes you match their pick. Sometimes you still like your own choice better. Either way, there's something to think about, and maybe something to argue about with friends.

07 | Share your result.
At the end, you get a score and a Share button. Send it to your draft group and see who made the same calls. A different pick doesn't automatically mean it was a bad one.

08 | 3 new Dailies. Every day.
There are three new Dailies every day. Draft Run, Powered Cube, and Latest Set. You can play one or try all three.

09 | Eight picks. Your call.
If you like drafting, give Pack One a try. Pick your cards, share your result, and tell me which decision you found the hardest. I'd really like to hear what you think.
