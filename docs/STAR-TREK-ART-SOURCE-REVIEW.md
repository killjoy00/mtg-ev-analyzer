## November 24, 2026 — full pre-archive source comparison

The [exact importer search](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/search?q=e%3Atrk&unique=prints&order=set) returned **586 unique printing IDs** over four pages. The [Arena English TRK search](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/search?q=set%3Atrk+game%3Aarena+lang%3Aen&unique=prints&order=set) returned 326 printing IDs over two pages, representing **311 distinct names**. The [Arena English SDS search](https://api.scryfall.com/cards/search?q=set%3Asds+game%3Aarena+lang%3Aen&unique=prints&order=set) returned 30 IDs in one page. All pages reconciled with their advertised totals. This is an inventory of Arena-available metadata, not a claim that every card occurs in Premier Draft.

- Replicated the existing main-set alias and printing rank against all 586 source printings; all 311 Arena names resolved to an Arena-available printing with the correct Oracle identity and HTTPS Scryfall normal image reference. No ambiguous aliases or unresolved names were found.
- All 311 TRK identities and 30 SDS identities have Oracle identity, type line, rarity, colors, color identity, valid printed mana symbols (empty on lands) and a normal artwork URL. The 15 additional Arena TRK printing records agree on identity, mana cost, colors, types, layout and rarity with their matching names. No mana-cost/printed-color discrepancies were found in the reviewed identities.
- All 30 SDS collector numbers, canonical names, player-facing titles and **complete image URLs** match the checked-in reference exactly; no SDS canonical or printed name collides with a TRK alias. All SDS printings are single-faced. TRK has 301 normal and 10 Saga layouts in the chosen source inventory; no split or double-face cases were observed. There are 37 TRK land identities and four SDS lands.
- The main-set resolver selects Arena-available artwork; decorative LCARS, promotional schematics and paper-only variants are not separate grading identities. The source data supplies legitimate punctuation-heavy names such as U.S.S. Enterprise-D, Galaxy-Class and Ni'Var Science Academy.
- No card metadata or scoring repair is indicated from this source review. The comparison exercised the resolver policy by reimplementing its ranking and alias rules against the retrieved records; it did not run the archive importer, load a database, or fetch/decode each individual image. Exact draft/game archive identity and observed image coverage remain the existing release-time check.

### Focused model compatibility notes (advisory)

- **Spacecraft:** 17 TRK artifact Spacecraft, including four with no colored mana cost (Class-F Shuttlecraft, Deep Space 9, U.S.S. Cerritos, Following Up, and U.S.S. Enterprise-D, Galaxy-Class). Existing metadata, printed-color and deck grouping rules can represent them. Station requirements and crew power are not explicit model features.
- **Alternative costs and named lands:** ten cards carry the beam me up keyword. Compare Open Communications and Pattern Buffer Preservation with Ensign Pavel Chekov and other repeatable enters payoffs. Check Cloak and Phaser, The Destruction of Praxis and The Traveler against pools with different named-land density; Planets and Anomalies (including Temporal Anomaly and Unexplored Territory) have materially different fixing roles. Printed mana value and observed affinity alone do not encode these interactions. November 20 public aggregate observations are contextual evidence, not official model inputs.
- **Modal, artifact and Federation context:** use Command Decision and Seven of Nine for modal-payoff examples; Hoshi Sato, Exolinguist for creature-type diversity; and Locutus, the One Who Speaks for artifact-creature synergy. These are watch cases, not new scoring rules or manually approved puzzles.
- The existing v6 model learns identity, pick/stage behavior, pair co-occurrence and deck color affinity. Rare Stardates can have insufficient evidence for a color or pair judgment. No new model, penalty, hard-coded card ranking, launch gate or manual puzzle review is proposed. See existing [land context](https://github.com/killjoy00/mtg-ev-analyzer/issues/1567) and [nonland context](https://github.com/killjoy00/mtg-ev-analyzer/issues/1568) investigations.
