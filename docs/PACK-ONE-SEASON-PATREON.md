# Season-triggered Patreon posts (draft implementation)

Tracks #1795 and #1796. This PR builds private data selection and branded
rendering; **not an enabled scheduler or verified publication path**. No named
production content is generated by CI. The owner-paired Monday publisher is
the only supported target, not the retired database-login setup.

## Season Results & Awards

Read the persisted outgoing season joined to its strictly newer successor and
the reconciliation watermark. On the successor's Pacific game date, after noon
Central, collect all three final season boards using the existing rating
policy and corpus lineage. No past season catch-up; an explicit enable-from
date is mandatory. A temporary older-set fallback is not a season close.
Rank and retain every tie through fifth; highlight rank one in blue in the
fixed Draft Run / Powered Cube / Latest Set order. The only additional award
is most distinct perfect (100-point) Dailies within each format; include all
ties, omit empty awards. No fabricated strategic awards or forced commentary.

Names are resolved by the existing private name admission helper, with
anonymous replacements where necessary. The result includes private placement
bookkeeping; never log, email from CI, commit, or upload that snapshot.

## Midseason Set Guide

The current persisted season has no end date until the successor actually
starts. Supply a reviewed next-regular-set planned start and explicit
enable-from date. Generate on the midpoint Pacific game date, after noon
Central (ceil half of the planned duration); cover only completed dates.
Do not guess a date from a release rumor or backfill after a missed milestone.
Plan changes must not create another season key.

Reuse the existing non-QA, deduplicated first-answer aggregate SQL. Qualify
only actual Latest Set decisions from the named set, real card art and full
prior picks, at least 50 answers per decision, at least 24 qualifying decisions
over at least seven days, 70% coverage of elapsed dates and of unique scheduled
decisions, and eight decisions per stage (P1P1–3 vs P1P4+). Distinguish pooled
response shares from per-decision model/trophy agreement. Example packs are
chosen from different days and stages; no names or raw player IDs.

For game outcomes use a fresh reviewed same-set 17Lands Premier Draft all-users,
no-splashes color snapshot, at most seven days old as of the completed-day
cutoff, with a verified same-sample baseline. At least eight of ten combinations
need 500 games; omit thin combinations. Do not call color results archetype
rankings or imply causation. The Star Trek draft guide is not a fresh approved
midseason results snapshot. If evidence is insufficient, do not generate an
article. The first guide remains an editorial-review draft.
