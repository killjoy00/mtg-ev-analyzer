Activation

Patreon handles the membership. Pack One handles the unlock.

Joining Patreon and connecting Patreon to Pack One are two separate steps. The connection is what lets Pack One verify the Elite tier on your account.

01Use a free Pack One account

Elite benefits attach to your Pack One account, so sign in or create one first.

02Join or upgrade on Patreon

Choose the Elite membership on Patreon. Patreon remains the place where membership and billing are managed.

03Connect Patreon

Authorize the Patreon account you used for Elite. Pack One checks the connected membership and unlocks premium practice when Elite is active.
