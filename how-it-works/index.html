Game guide

How Pack One works

Play eight decisions, match the trophy drafter, and compare your score.

Advertisement

Choose a Daily.

Start Daily Draft Run or Daily Powered Cube. Each contains eight independent decisions from different qualified trophy drafts. Read the real pack and earlier pool, choose a card, and lock your pick.

See what happened.

The reveal shows the trophy drafter’s actual choice and model support. Matching the trophy pick earns 100. Other choices earn up to 95. Your result averages all eight scores.

Everyone gets the same Daily.

Both Dailies reset at midnight Eastern. Each is fixed for that date, with no rerolls. Resume your saved attempt if you leave. Anonymous limits use best-effort browser/device identity; authenticated accounts have one authoritative attempt per Daily across devices.

Recent sets, with room for history.

New regular Dailies have at least two decisions from the newest Live eligible set and at least four sampled from the three releases before it. Remaining decisions favor recent releases while keeping older sets possible. Regular rounds cover Picks 1–8. Cube starts at its archive’s first complete pack, Pick 2, and finishes at Pick 9, with the actual first card visible.

Share your result.

A Daily result links to the universal Daily entry and never creates a private Daily. Share a practice run so a friend can play the exact same eight decisions and compare scores.

Keep playing.

After both Dailies, a free account can start another regular random Draft Run. Accounts enable durable leaderboard participation. Guests can complete and share without appearing as an identified leaderboard entry; signing in later does not retroactively rank that result. Unshared practice may have rerolls; fixed shared runs do not.

Put it into practice

Make the decision before you read the answer.

Pack One is most useful when you commit first, then use the consensus as a comparison point—not an instruction sheet.
