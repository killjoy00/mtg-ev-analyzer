Lorwyn Eclipsed

A quick refresher before your next pick.

Format reminders

  • Five creature-type themes anchor the format; changelings connect them.
  • Build a dependable two-color base before considering a splash.
  • Check creature types as well as individual card strength.

Archetypes

The main starting points for this format. Follow the cards available to you.

WUMerfolkTap creatures for value.

Convoke turns a developed board into mana.

BRGoblinsUse blight and death triggers.

Expendable creatures help absorb the cost of your payoffs.

GWKithkinBuild a wide board.

Team bonuses reward keeping several creatures in play.

URElementalsCast spells with mana value four or more.

Ramp helps reach the expensive spells that reward this deck.

BGElvesFill and reuse your graveyard.

Self-mill supports creature recursion and graveyard payoffs.

Mechanics at a glance
Changelings
These creatures have every creature type.
Blight
Put the specified number of −1/−1 counters on one creature you control.
Vivid
Counts the colors among permanents you control, not the colors of mana you can produce.
