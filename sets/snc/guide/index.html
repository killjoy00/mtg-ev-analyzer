Streets of New Capenna

A quick refresher before your next pick.

Format reminders

  • Five families overlap through allied-color pairs.
  • Start with a reliable base before stretching to three colors.
  • Fixing is part of the deck, not a final detail.

Archetypes

The main starting points for this format. Follow the cards available to you.
