Set guide · DSK

Duskmourn: House of Horror

A quick refresher before your next pick.

Format reminders

  • Enchantments and Rooms enable eerie.
  • Delirium needs different card types in your graveyard.
  • Manifest dread helps develop the board while filling the graveyard.

Archetypes

The main starting points for this format. Follow the cards available to you.

UBEerie controlAccumulate enchantment value.
