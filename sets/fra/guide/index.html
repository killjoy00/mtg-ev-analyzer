Reality Fracture

A quick refresher before your next pick.

Format reminders

  • Ten two-color archetypes: ally pairs follow Hexhaven’s colleges, enemy pairs follow major characters.
  • Empower Jace adds loyalty to one Jace token that surveils or draws cards.
  • Heartwood tokens are mana artifacts that let red-green ramp early.

Mechanics at a glance
Empower Jace
Put loyalty counters on your Jace token, creating one first if needed. It can surveil 1 or draw a card.
Heartwood
A red and green artifact token that taps for red or green mana.
Prepare
A prepared creature lets you cast a copy of its attached spell; doing so unprepares it.
Flashback
Cast the instant or sorcery from your graveyard for its flashback cost, then exile it.
