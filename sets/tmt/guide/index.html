Set guide · TMT

Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles

A quick refresher before your next pick.

Format reminders

  • Five enemy-color pairs define the main themes.
  • Cheap evasive creatures enable sneak.
  • Artifact tokens can serve several different synergies.

Archetypes

The main starting points for this format. Follow the cards available to you.

WBSneaky NinjasConnect with evasive attackers.

Sneak reuses creatures while deploying new threats.

URMachinesBuild around artifacts.

Artifact creatures and tokens supply your payoffs.

BGDisappearHave your permanents leave play.

Sacrifice and sneak can both enable disappear.

RWAllianceKeep creatures entering.

Tokens and blink effects add more triggers.

GUMutagenGrow creatures with counters.

Mutagen tokens connect artifact and counter synergies.
