Tarkir: Dragonstorm

A quick refresher before your next pick.

Format reminders

  • Five three-color clans share overlapping two-color bases.
  • Draft fixing alongside spells, not after them.
  • Choose expensive Dragons your mana can actually cast.

Archetypes

The main starting points for this format. Follow the cards available to you.
