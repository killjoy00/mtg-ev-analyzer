What's in this sample?

This page covers 300 drafts. The model for this set learned from 209,999 picks across 5,000 drafts, taken from a pool of 40,480 drafts by players with at least a 60% win rate.

How close were the first picks?

On average, the model's top card had 27.9 points more support than its second choice. In 24% of packs the gap was 10 points or less, and in 42% it was 30 or more. That matters when you miss one. Losing a coin flip isn't the same as passing the card the model thought was clearly best.

How to use this page

If a set has lots of close first picks, expect to disagree with the model more often. That doesn't mean you drafted badly. These numbers only describe these 300 drafts and this version of the model, not every card or every possible pack.
