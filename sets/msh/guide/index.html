A quick refresher before your next pick.

Format reminders

  • Ten color pairs offer distinct plans.
  • Creature types, counters, and artifact tokens overlap.
  • Balance synergy pieces with interaction and a usable curve.

The main starting points for this format. Follow the cards available to you.
