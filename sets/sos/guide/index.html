Set guide · SOS

Secrets of Strixhaven

A quick refresher before your next pick.

Format reminders

  • Five colleges each support an enemy-color pair.
  • Spell sequencing matters as much as spell size.
  • Keep enough creatures to exploit your spell-based rewards.

Archetypes

The main starting points for this format. Follow the cards available to you.

WBSilverquill reparteeTarget creatures and attack.

Pair cheap threats with targeted spells.

URPrismari opusInvest mana in spells.

Opus upgrades at five mana spent, not five mana value.

BGWitherbloom infusionGain life and build a swarm.

Pest tokens support lifegain and sacrifice.

RWLorehold flashbackReuse the graveyard.

Flashback also enables rewards for cards leaving it.

GUQuandrix incrementGrow creatures with larger spells.

Increment compares spell mana value with power or toughness.
