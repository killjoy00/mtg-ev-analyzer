What is in the SOS sample?

This archive uses 300 historical replay seats. The consensus model for this set was trained from 5,000 drafts and 210,000 picks drawn from an experienced cohort of 70,209 drafts. The recorded win-rate cutoff for the cohort is 60%.

How decisive were the opening packs?

Across these replay seats, the model's average gap between its first and second cards was 28.8%. 26% of packs had a gap of ten support points or less, while 45% had a gap of thirty points or more. That split is useful when reviewing a miss: a narrow disagreement is not the same signal as passing a card the model strongly separated from the field.

How to use this page

Use the statistics to calibrate your expectations before treating a score as a verdict. A set with many close opening decisions should naturally produce more reasonable disagreement. The archive is descriptive of the stored replay sample and model version; it is not a complete ranking of every card or every possible pack.

Put it into practice

Make the decision before you read the answer.

Pack One is most useful when you commit first, then use the consensus as a comparison point—not an instruction sheet.
