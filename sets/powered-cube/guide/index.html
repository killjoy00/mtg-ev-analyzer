A quick refresher before your next pick.

Format reminders

  • A curated singleton environment: build a plan around the powerful cards available.
  • Fast mana and cheap interaction can matter more than ordinary Limited curves.
  • Cube lists change; check the card pool rather than assuming a combo is present.

Archetypes

Broad starting plans, not a fixed ten-pair archetype map. Colors can overlap.

UBlue controlCombine card advantage and interaction.

Draft a way to stabilize and eventually win.

BReanimatorBring a large threat back early.

You need discard or mill, a target, and reanimation.

RRed aggroPressure with efficient threats and burn.

Favor a dependable curve over scattered expensive cards.

GGreen rampAccelerate into game-ending threats.

Balance mana sources with meaningful payoffs.

WUArtifact enginesBuild around artifact synergies.

Prioritize support for the engine you actually drafted.
