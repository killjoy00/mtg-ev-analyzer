Set guide · TRK

Star Trek

A quick refresher before your next pick.

What to know about this set

  • Star Trek has ten two-color draft plans, with two multicolor uncommon signposts for each pair. Build a strong early board and keep efficient interaction; the November 15 early-format report measured about 8.65 turns per game.
  • Stardates reprints can appear in Star Trek Draft boosters. For example, the Stardates printing of Sheoldred, the Apocalypse is titled Khan, Engineered Evil. Pack One preserves the underlying card identity.
  • Useful nonbasic lands can be worth picking earlier when they support your mana and differently named land payoffs. A November 20 card-level snapshot highlights the strength of Cloak and Phaser and The Traveler; do not sacrifice reliable early mana just to add another land name.

Archetypes

These are the ten two-color plans identified by Wizards for Star Trek Draft. They describe intended synergies, not observed win rates or a ranking.

WUSpacecraft and ArtificersStation and power up Spacecraft.

Use creatures with enters abilities to develop the board and help station your ships; protect the time you invest in charging them.

UBDilemma ControlChoose modes and control the game.

Play modal spells that answer threats, then use payoffs for facing a dilemma.

BRKlingons and SacrificeAttack with expendable creatures.

Generate hasty Klingon Warriors and turn deaths or sacrifices into extra value.

RGAway Team LandfallReward extra land drops.

Landfall creatures and explorers make every land drop more valuable; keep deploying lands while pressuring the opponent.

GWDoctors and CountersDevelop creatures with counters.

Build around +1/+1 counters and cards that reward any kind of counter, including charge counters on Spacecraft.

WBBorg Artifact CreaturesBuild a board of artifacts.

Use artifact creatures and Borg payoffs; sacrifice expendable artifacts when they can produce a stronger effect.

URScientists and NoncreaturesTrigger noncreature-spell rewards.

Mix inexpensive spells with noncreature artifacts, including Spacecraft, to keep Scientists generating value.

BGArchaeology GraveyardStock and reuse the graveyard.

Surveil, recur creatures or artifacts, and use graveyard interactions and beam me up to gain extra value.

RWCommand and Eight PowerBuild a wide, powerful crew.

Develop enough board presence to reach eight total creature power, then benefit from command payoffs and efficient attacks.

GUStrange New WorldsPlay differently named lands.

Prioritize useful Planets, Anomalies and other distinct land names. Different names matter more than extra copies; balance the payoff against reliable colored mana.

Mechanics at a glance
Beam me up
Cast an eligible instant or sorcery from your graveyard for the listed alternate cost and return a creature you control to its owner's hand. Exile the spell afterward; its printed mana value is unchanged.
Face a dilemma
You face a dilemma when you choose one or more modes for a modal spell or ability; choosing multiple modes still counts as one dilemma.
Federation
Rewards the diversity of creature types among your non-Borg creatures. The exact reward depends on the card.
Strange new worlds
Counts your lands with different names, not how many copies of one land you have.
Spacecraft / station
At sorcery speed, tap another untapped creature you control (even one that just entered) to add charge counters equal to its power. Thresholds unlock abilities and may turn the Spacecraft into a creature; charge counters are not +1/+1 counters.
Assimilate
Put the specified card onto the battlefield under your control, or take control of it if already there, with a +1/+1 counter. It becomes a Borg artifact creature and loses its other creature types.
