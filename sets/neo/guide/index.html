Set guide · NEO

Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty

A quick refresher before your next pick.

Format reminders

  • Artifacts and enchantments create overlapping synergies.
  • Sagas and channel cards offer flexibility.
  • Cheap evasive creatures enable ninjutsu.
