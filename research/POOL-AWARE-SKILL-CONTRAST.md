# Skill-aware conditional choice contrast v1 — development protocol

Issue #1863. Owner approved trying skill-aware scoring after the opportunity-only challenger failed to demonstrate improved scoring separation in the complete ten-format historical evaluation (Mixed 0.559533 to 0.556553; Cube 0.606424 to 0.605101; both paired intervals contain zero). Keep v6 live.

## Hypothesis and fixed recipe

Fit two conditional multinomial choice models against the same frozen v6 support offset, using the existing six mana/opportunity features and regularized card-specific intercepts. One model learns choices by experienced strong drafters, the other by equally experienced ordinary drafters in the same residual time window. Condition both on the offered pack and previous pool. A card effect requires at least 100 offered observations in EACH residual cohort; otherwise zero. Both models use mean negative conditional log likelihood with context L2 penalty .02 and card L2 penalty 30 / min(cohort decision counts); the same penalty for both groups avoids asymmetric shrinkage from different cohort sizes. No validation hyperparameter search. Subtract ordinary coefficients from strong coefficients, including card effects, then center the resulting log-preference contrast by the unweighted mean across the actual pack. Candidate support = v6 support times exp(clip(0.5 * centered contrast, -ln(1.25), +ln(1.25))). This is a conservative anchored preference score, not a literal probability of choosing correctly. Equal cohort preference leaves the incumbent support unchanged. A card liked equally by both cohorts is not penalized merely for popularity. Each multiplier is bounded 0.8–1.25; ratio changes between two candidates at most 1.5625 before normalization and leader movement. These are researcher-chosen development bounds, not previously approved product tolerances. The unchanged linear award formula, 95 alternative cap, and 100 trophy override are preserved; the benchmark excludes the override.

## Identical data and validation

Use all ten completed formats (DFT, TDM, KTK, HOB, Cube, EOE, FIN, ECL, TMT, SOS). Restore exact earlier archive, metadata, windows, baseline cohort and source hashes; compare baseline fit counts, coverage and v6 validation metrics to each successful original report before fitting the new contrast. Preserve strict chronological baseline/residual/validation and whole-draft disjointness. The experienced strong threshold is established solely in the baseline window, with 100+ games and top-15% win-rate bucket. Ordinary training labels are restricted to the same residual window as strong training labels. No actual Pack One player records, later picks, source identity, event outcome or skill label enter a scoring feature. Within-skill win association is descriptive reporting only. Prior validation data have already informed research, so this is development evidence, not an untouched final test.

## Evaluation and stopping rule

Reuse the fixed product-profile simulation and paired source-draft bootstrap in the ten-format benchmark, including P1P2-opening support for ECL/TMT and separate Mixed/Cube reporting. Compare v6 and the single fixed contrast candidate on identical observed choices. Primary: positive paired AUC improvement interval in Mixed and Cube separately, with 10/10 format coverage. Guardrails: no per-set increase in strong-player awards below 25 exceeding 1 pp; upper paired raw log-loss delta <= .005; lower paired top-1 delta >= -1 pp. Report context proxy movements for missing access, supported colors, fixing and close alternatives, score bands, correction cap frequency, observed-cohort means, and within-skill event outcome associations. Review all results and limitations before any further assessment. Do not tune the fixed recipe against these results or treat a positive AUC as causal draft quality. If no meaningful improvement, stop this challenger and retain v6. No production changes, no historical regrades, no recurring service or new paid AI API, no changes to required CI gates.

Per-format aggregate reports include public card-name coefficients and fit diagnostics for reproducibility. One-day anonymous simulation banks have only local draft ordinal, skill cohort, pick position, difficulty and two model-only scores. Retain aggregate reports for 90 days. No private player data, source IDs, timestamps or outcomes are exported.
