## 4. Methodology and release note draft

**Proposed release wording:** Pack One's scoring model compares your pick with patterns in public drafts by experienced strong drafters. The revised model uses card-specific differences between strong and ordinary drafters and context preferences learned from strong drafters. Partial credit remains based on relative model support; matching the trophy drafter earns 100. The candidate better distinguishes strong from ordinary historical drafting choices and improves prediction of strong-player picks. It accounts more explicitly for the cards already in the pool. It does not establish that any particular alternative is objectively better, and actual match-win association is essentially unchanged. These are historical development comparisons, with five formats used for design selection, 24/26 positive independent-of-design format intervals, and eight legacy archives lacking game completion timestamps. Sets without a validated fit keep the previous scoring model. No historical score is rewritten.
