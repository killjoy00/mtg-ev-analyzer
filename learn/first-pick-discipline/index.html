Rank your top three.

Before you see what the trophy drafter took, rank the three cards you'd seriously consider. Don't just identify the bomb rare and the best removal spell. Decide which one you'd actually take and how close you think the choice is. Settling the order before the reveal keeps you honest about what you really thought.

How close do you think it is?

You can like one card more than another and still think it's a close pick. If the model sees a huge difference where you saw a toss-up, that's worth looking into. The reverse is true too.

Look for the kind of mistake, not just the card.

After a miss, ask what caused it. Did you overrate a card's raw stats? Underrate flexibility? Chase synergy before you had a reason to? Misjudge a hard-to-cast card? The lesson that carries over is usually a kind of mistake, not “remember that this card is better.”

A routine that helps: rank three, say how sure you are, reveal, then write one sentence about the biggest difference between your ranking and the model's.

Practice without memorizing.

Unlimited runs help until you start recognizing the exact packs. When that happens, switch sets or come back later. You want practice making the decision, not an answer key.
