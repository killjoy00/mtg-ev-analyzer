# Premier versus Traditional: protocol fixed before measurement

This research does not change production evidence or playable puzzles. The null is that qualified Premier and Traditional trophy drafters use the same first-eight pick policy after controlling for set, pick and pool.

Predeclared sets: BLB, DFT, FIN and HOB, spanning several release periods. Official public draft and game archives must exist for both PremierDraft and TradDraft. Missing schema, outcome or skill evidence is reported as a coverage limitation; no substitute inferred qualification is allowed. Each archive is pinned by checksum/ETag in the output. No set is selected because its result favors pooling.

Eligibility retains the existing standard: at least 100 games, the upper 15% of experienced win-rate buckets with a floor of 0.60, computed within event/set. Premier trophies are 7 wins and 0–2 losses; Traditional trophies are 3 wins and 0 losses. Selection rates and cutoffs are reported separately because bucket fields do not establish identical player identities across formats.

Only complete, validated P1P1–P1P8 trajectories enter the experiment. Splits are deterministic by source draft (60% train, 15% validation, 25% test). A source can never cross partitions. Training uses at most 5,000 trophy drafts per event/set, sampled by stable hash; combined training includes both groups. All models use the frozen context implementation, with card-pair expectations and color/deck-fit evidence estimated exclusively from their training drafts. Only observed first-match, first-game decks inform the color fit in both formats; later sideboard changes cannot manufacture an event distinction. Game outcomes establish eligibility, not inference features.

Four configurations: Premier-only; Traditional-only; combined; combined plus explicit event type. The event-feature model interpolates combined and event-conditional log supports with a single validation-selected weight in {0, .25, .5, .75, 1}. Weight zero removes event information. Each model's probability exponent is selected on validation from its training event(s) only, from {1, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 2, 2.5}. Final test decisions are never used to choose either hyperparameter.

Report raw and calibrated held-out log loss, top-1 accuracy, mean rank and ten-bin top-choice calibration for each training configuration on each target event, per set and pooled. Paired source-draft bootstrap intervals use 500 draws. A set needs at least 100 training and 50 test drafts per event for a pooling conclusion. At least three sufficient sets are required; otherwise the verdict is inconclusive.

Pooling is supported only if, on both held-out event types, the upper 95% bound of combined-minus-same-event log loss is at most 2% of baseline log loss, top-1 decline is no worse than 1 percentage point, and cross-event log loss is within 5% of same-event prediction. Event-type improvement must have an upper 95% bound below 1% of combined log loss. These are product noninferiority tolerances, not proof that policies are identical.

Residuals use the same set, card, pick range (1–2 / 3–5 / 6–8), and pool color-commitment band. Compare observed choice minus combined-model probability only in cells with at least 20 candidate appearances from each event. Report card and category residuals, opportunity counts and source-clustered uncertainty. Category labels come from disclosed card-text/type heuristics for fixing, multicolor, archetype payoffs, synergy, build-arounds, removal, bombs, sideboard-oriented, aggressive and controlling cards; they are exploratory proxies. A persistent pattern requires the same direction in at least three sets with ≥3 percentage-point residual difference and intervals excluding zero. Individual noisy cards cannot establish policy differences. Unclassified/low-support coverage is reported.

Decisions are separate: (1) whether Traditional may contribute model evidence, and (2) whether Traditional trophy trajectories may become playable puzzles. No automatic production pooling or publication follows this report. Insufficient power or failed criteria retain Premier-only production.
