About Pack One

Eight decisions from real trophy drafts.

Advertisement

Make a pick, then compare.

The idea for Pack One came from a Reddit post about studying trophy drafts to get better at Limited. I thought it would be fun to see the same packs successful drafters did, make my own picks, and then compare. So I built a daily game around eight decisions from eight different trophy drafts.

Three free Dailies.

Every day there are three challenges: Draft Run, Powered Cube, and Latest Set. Everyone gets the same picks. You don't need an account to play or share your results. A free account lets you join the leaderboard and play as many regular practice runs as you want.

How scoring works

Matching the trophy drafter gets you 100 points. Other picks can score up to 95 if the model supports them. That doesn't mean every other card was wrong.

Built by one person, with AI.

Pack One is a one-person project. I'm not a programmer, and I couldn't have built it without AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT. I've used them extensively to write code, solve technical problems, and turn ideas into working features.

But the vision and decisions are mine.

The game itself is grounded in real data. Every puzzle comes from an actual recorded draft, and scoring uses a statistical model based on real drafting behavior. Neither the model nor the trophy drafter is right every time.

AI made it possible for one Magic player without a coding background to build something I wanted to play. I'm responsible for what ends up in Pack One, including its mistakes, and I'm always working to make it better.

The site has also been improved by feedback from Magic players like you, so don’t hesitate to let me know your suggestions for improvements.
