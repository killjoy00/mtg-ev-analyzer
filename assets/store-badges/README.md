## Links and display

- iPhone: `https://apps.apple.com/app/id6814318676`
- Android: `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=pro.packone.app`

Use the files *as supplied*: no recoloring, hand-drawn approximations, cropping of the actual badge, or modified copy. The Google PNG is 646 × 250 with built-in transparent padding; the homepage uses its original artwork, scaled to present an approximately 40px-tall visible badge. The Apple SVG appears first at 40px. Maintain at least 10px clear space around a 40px visible badge; the homepage adds extra spacing between badges.

Official brand guides: [Apple App Store Marketing and Identity Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/) · [Google Play Partner Marketing Hub](https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-play/google-play/lockups-icons-badges/).

To update these assets in the future, obtain fresh files **directly** from the vendor and update this provenance record and hashes. Do not copy badges from unrelated GitHub repositories.
