# Pack One store download badges

These are English black-background download badges kept here for reuse in Pack One web/marketing work. The assets are unchanged SVG copies of the Apple and Google Play artwork, obtained from public GitHub-hosted copies of their badge files; check the linked brand pages for the most recent official revision before final publication.

- `app-store-en-black.svg` (Apple English/US black badge; file originally named `Download_on_the_App_Store_Badge_US-UK_RGB_blk_092917.svg`). Public file source: https://github.com/RefugeRestrooms/refugerestrooms-redesign-web-mockup/blob/master/Download_on_the_App_Store_Badge_US-UK_RGB_blk_092917.svg . Official resources: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/ .
- `google-play-en.svg` (Google Play English badge). Public file source: https://github.com/RefugeRestrooms/refugerestrooms-redesign-web-mockup/blob/master/Google-Play-Badge.svg . Official resources: https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-play/google-play/lockups-icons-badges/ .

Store listing URLs (verified in `worker/mobile-version.mjs`):

- iPhone/iPad: `https://apps.apple.com/app/id6814318676`
- Android: `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=pro.packone.app`

Keep the badges unmodified, with sufficient contrast and clear space. Apple requires its black badge first alongside another app-store badge and at least 40 px tall on screen. Google recommends its own badge be at least 28 px tall and as large as or larger than other app-store badges. If a source has changed, replace the entire file with the official current asset rather than manually editing its artwork.
